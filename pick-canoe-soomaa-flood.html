Soomaa fifth-season flooded-forest canoeing

Floodwater transforms the forest into a temporary canoe landscape. This is a genuinely event-led discovery: the attraction depends on a natural water-level trigger rather than an annual opening date.

What it is

A naturally triggered flood window creates an outing that cannot be scheduled from a fixed annual opening date.

When to go

Typical conditions: Mar, Apr. Operator describes March-April depending on weather; flood occurrence is not guaranteed.

Getting there

Starts from: Soomaa National Park, Estonia. The operator sets the exact pier or meeting place – confirm it when you book.

Plan around

Launch conditions, exposure, route and supervision require local confirmation.

Local conditions

Daylight: about 18 hours in late June and 6 hours in late December.
