Choose a whale destination for the species and time of year, not only the overall scenic score. Resident populations, migrations and an operator timetable are different evidence layers.

Sightings are never guaranteed. A departure may be cancelled, and conduct rules still apply during a strong wildlife season.

27 researched candidates in this edition. Method: setting 40%, distinctiveness 35%, experience depth 25%, each assessed 0 to 4. Equal scores genuinely tie. These are subjective editorial assessments, not visitor ratings or safety certifications.
