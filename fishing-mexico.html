Licence and rules

Who needs what

  • Sport and recreational fishing needs a permit from CONAPESCA, with the gear the authority allows.

Prices

Sailfish Pez vela · Istiophorus platypterus

Sea Pacific coast – Ixtapa, Puerto Vallarta, and Cancún in spring.

How to recognise it

  • Huge sail-shaped dorsal fin
  • Long bill
  • Blue-black back with light bars

When

JFMAMJJASOND

Time of day: Daytime offshore.

Bait and method

  • Trolling: ballyhoo and teasers; circle hooks and release

Blue marlin Marlín azul · Makaira nigricans

Sea Cabo San Lucas, Cozumel.

How to recognise it

  • Cobalt-blue back with pale bars
  • Long spear-like bill
  • One of the largest bony fish

When

JFMAMJJASOND

Time of day: Daytime in blue water.

Bait and method

  • Trolling: big lures at speed; tag-and-release is standard

Fact file

Mahi-mahi (dorado) Coryphaena hippurus

Sea Both coasts.

How to recognise it

  • Brilliant green-gold with blue spots
  • Bulls have a steep forehead
  • Long dorsal fin from head to tail

When

JFMAMJJASOND

Time of day: Daytime around floating weed and debris.

Bait and method

  • Trolling: small lures and ballyhoo near weed lines
  • Casting: jigs to schools at the boat

Fact file

Roosterfish Pez gallo · Nematistius pectoralis

Sea Baja California Sur beaches.

How to recognise it

  • Long comb-like dorsal fin ("rooster comb")
  • Dark curved stripes
  • Pacific coast of the Americas

When

JFMAMJJASOND

Time of day: Morning along surf beaches.

Bait and method

  • Live bait: slow-trolled live baits
  • Casting: poppers along the beach – released by custom

Fact file

Yellowfin tuna Atún aleta amarilla · Thunnus albacares

Sea Baja and Pacific offshore.

How to recognise it

  • Dark blue back with a yellow side stripe
  • Long yellow second dorsal and anal fins
  • Bright yellow finlets

When

JFMAMJJASOND

Time of day: Dawn feeding frenzies with birds.

Bait and method

  • Trolling: skirted lures and cedar plugs
  • Popping: big poppers into surface schools

Fact file

Bonefish Macabí · Albula vulpes

Sea Ascensión Bay and Chetumal.

How to recognise it

  • Silver, almost transparent "grey ghost" of the flats
  • Pointed snout, underslung mouth
  • Very fast first run

When

JFMAMJJASOND

Time of day: Rising and falling tides on shallow flats.

Bait and method

  • Fly: small shrimp patterns cast to tailing fish – almost always released

Fact file

Permit Palometa · Trachinotus falcatus

Sea Yucatán flats.

How to recognise it

  • Deep, silver, almost round body
  • Long black sickle fins
  • Shy fish of the flats

When

JFMAMJJASOND

Time of day: Moving tides on flats and wrecks.

Bait and method

  • Fly: crab patterns – one of the hardest fish to catch on fly
  • Spinning: live crabs on wrecks

Fact file

Tarpon Sábalo · Megalops atlanticus

Sea and rivers Yucatán lagoons.

How to recognise it

  • Huge silver scales
  • Upturned mouth
  • Spectacular jumps

When

JFMAMJJASOND

Time of day: Dawn and dusk; nights in passes.

Bait and method

  • Fly: large streamers to rolling fish
  • Live bait: crabs and mullet in the passes

Fact file

Largemouth bass Lobina negra · Micropterus salmoides

Freshwater El Salto and Baccarac lakes.

How to recognise it

  • Jaw reaches past the eye
  • Dark broken band along the side
  • Deep notch between the two dorsal fins

When

JFMAMJJASOND

Time of day: Dawn and dusk near weeds and wood.

Bait and method

  • Soft plastics: worms and creature baits around cover
  • Topwater: poppers and frogs at first light
