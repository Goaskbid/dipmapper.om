  1. Hermanus | South Africa

    #1 Hermanus whale watching

    Editorial score 94/100. Equal scores share rank; list order is not a measured gap.

    A boat trip into Walker Bay with species-specific calendars. The main southern-right-whale period differs from the shorter humpback window and the possible year-round presence of Brydes whales.

    Why go: Walker Bay offers several whale themes with different calendars. The species-specific distinction is the reason to choose a dated outing rather than a generic year-round whale promise.

    Key attractions: southern right whale / humpback whale / brydes whale / Whale watching

    Season and visit timing

    Southern right whale: Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec. Southern right whales use the bay for mating, calving and nursing; December is a shoulder period. Season source

    Humpback whale: Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep. Humpback presence has a shorter published window. Season source

    Brydes whale: All months. Resident Brydes whales may be encountered; this does not imply year-round tour operation. Season source

    Operator program: Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov. Core advertised programme ends in November; ask about December departures. Season source

    Plan around: No sighting guarantee; distinguish species season from boat operation and wildlife conduct.

    Tickets and published costs

    ZAR 1580 / adult_trip. published_reference. Do not roll the 2026 amount into 2027 automatically. Reference date: 2026-11-12. Not a live or all-in quote. Price source
