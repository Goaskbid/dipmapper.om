      How it works

      DipMapper opens around your current search radius, keeps the map visible, and shows useful swim choices first. Tap Open on a place, or tap a numbered map dot, for photos, weather, prices, facilities, parking and source links in one travel card.

      Close first
      Nearest good options first, then wider only if needed.
      Useful facts
      Free, family fit, supervision, WC, food, indoor/outdoor and water feel.
      Source links
      Operator, map, photo, weather and review links are easy to open from the card.

      About DipMapper

      Why DipMapper exists

      “A good place to swim should be easier to find.” DipMapper started as a way to find a lake bath, beach or thermal pool near home without ten browser tabs – and grew into a planner for swimming, paddling, diving and boat trips with live tides, official water quality and wildlife seasons.
