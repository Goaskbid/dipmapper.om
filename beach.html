Beach discoveries

Compare shore settings and seasonal context, then decide whether your visit means swimming, shore viewing or a supervised water activity. These are deliberately different uses of a beach.

Local flags, surf, water quality, tides and facilities take priority over the editorial score.

37 researched candidates in this edition. Method: setting 40%, distinctiveness 35%, experience depth 25%, each assessed 0 to 4. Equal scores genuinely tie. These are subjective editorial assessments, not visitor ratings or safety certifications.

Publisher-approved edition. Typical seasons and indoor visit formats are not live forecasts or opening guarantees. Source dates and ticket categories remain part of each card.
