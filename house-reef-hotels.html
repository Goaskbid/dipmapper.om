Before you book

  • Ask how you get in: beach entry, jetty ladder or a channel through the reef flat – and at what tide.
  • Many resorts set snorkelling hours, require a buddy or ask you to stay inside marked zones; some charge for guided house-reef dives.
  • Currents along an outer reef can be strong – the dive centre on site knows the safe entry points of the day.
  • Reef-safe sun protection, no touching, no standing on coral. See diving rules and conditions at a glance.
