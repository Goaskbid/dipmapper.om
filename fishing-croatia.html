Licence and rules

Who needs what

  • Recreational sea fishing needs a licence whether you fish from the shore or a boat: one day, several days or a year.
  • Fresh water has its own separate permits.

Where to get it

  • Daily and multi-day licences online (confirmation by email), at fisheries offices or authorised sellers.
  • Annual licences are sold only from 1 December to 1 March.

Rules to know

  • Up to two handlines, trolling lines or squid jigs; from a boat a rod of up to 30 lb.
  • National parks, nature parks and special reserves need special licences.

Source: gov.hr – Marine recreational and sport fisheries

EU sea-angling registration

Since 10 January 2026 the EU requires recreational sea anglers who target species under EU recreational conservation measures to register and to report those catches daily by phone app – whatever your nationality. It runs through the RecFishing app in Belgium, Cyprus, Denmark, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Malta, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania and Sweden, and through national systems in Bulgaria, Croatia, Estonia, Finland, Greece, Latvia, Lithuania, Slovenia and Spain. It does not replace any national licence.

Source: European Commission – Questions and answers on RecFishing (12 Jan 2026)

Brown trout Potočna pastrva · Salmo trutta

Freshwater Gacka, Kupa.

How to recognise it

  • Round red spots with pale rims, plus black spots
  • Adipose fin, often with an orange edge
  • Golden-brown flanks

When

JFMAMJJASOND

Time of day: Afternoon and evening best.

Bait and method

  • Spinning: small spinners and wobblers 4–7 cm; in streams cast across the current and let the lure swing
  • Float or ledger: worms, bee maggots, mealworms
  • Fly: dry flies and nymphs – where fly fishing is allowed

Pike Štuka · Esox lucius

Freshwater Danube backwaters.

How to recognise it

  • Long body with the dorsal fin set far back near the tail
  • Long, flat "duck-bill" snout full of strong teeth
  • Green with pale, bean-shaped spots

When

JFMAMJJASOND

Time of day: Morning and evening best, afternoons good.

Bait and method

  • Spinning: wobblers, spinners and spinnerbaits, soft jig lures
  • Float or ledger: dead baitfish – in winter on or near the bottom; live baitfish are banned on Lake Lucerne and in free angling
  • Free angling: a single barbless hook and no baitfish – real pike fishing needs a cantonal permit

Quick answers

Do I need a fishing licence in Croatia?

Fresh water: Separate freshwater permits. Sea: Licence – even from the shore. Recreational sea fishing needs a licence whether you fish from the shore or a boat: one day, several days or a year.

Where do I buy a fishing licence in Croatia?

Daily and multi-day licences online (confirmation by email), at fisheries offices or authorised sellers.
